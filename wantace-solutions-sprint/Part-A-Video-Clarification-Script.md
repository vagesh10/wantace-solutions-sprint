# Part A — Honest Video Wording

> **Important:** Use this wording instead of claiming that a real interview took place. The available information is secondary research, not a completed first-hand audit.

## Suggested spoken section (about 45–60 seconds)

“For Part A, I researched a publicly documented tailoring enterprise called Sampurna Tailoring, profiled by the Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India. The public profile names Pramila Naik and reports a monthly turnover of ₹32,000. It also describes an expansion into fancy dresses and other clothing.

However, I was unable to complete the assignment’s required 20-minute interview with the owner or manager. The public source does not tell me how orders arrive, which tools are used to track them, what the most frequent operational problem is, or what that problem costs. It also does not provide two direct quotes or photographs of the actual tracking methods.

For that reason, I have not invented quotes, order numbers, photos, or observations. Any process flow I show is only an illustrative hypothesis to validate during a real interview, not an observed workflow. I would complete the audit by speaking with the owner, following one order from enquiry to payment, and asking permission before taking photos or sharing contact details.”

## Source

Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India (EDII), “Pramila Naik – Sampurna Tailoring”: https://ediindia.org/project-programme-st/pramila-naik-sampurna-tailoring/

## Do not say

- “I interviewed the owner” unless that interview really occurred.
- Any quotation attributed to the owner unless the owner actually said those words and you recorded them accurately.
- A specific order volume, current tracking tool, recurring issue, cost estimate, or process step unless it is supported by the source or verified directly.
- That illustrative workflow diagrams or generic photos are photos of the real business.
